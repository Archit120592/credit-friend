Four steps to a set that lasts

Stones and charms fall off when they sit on wet top coat or get buried under it. This order keeps them secure and still sparkling.

01

Prep

Shape the nail, push back cuticles and wipe with a dehydrator so the base grips.

02

Base

Brush a thin base coat and cure it under your lamp. Thin coats cure evenly.

03

Place

Dab gel glue, pick each stone with a wax pencil and press it down flat. Cure.

04

Seal

Run no-wipe top coat around the stones, not over their faces, then cure again.
